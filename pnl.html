    Where the numbers come from

    • Monthly paper trade: NSE F&O bhavcopy, the official daily closes. It sells at the close of the month's first trading day; a stop exit fills at the next day's open.
    • Weekly paper trades (v3 and LEG-20): NSE's live option chain, one snapshot a minute on expiry days, taken from the owner's PC. NSE gives price snapshots, not candles: a minute's close is the snapshot at :50 seconds, a "next minute's open" fill is the next snapshot, and the 15:15 exit uses the 15:14:50 snapshot. These rows are marked "NSE snapshot".
    • How a day's P&L is counted: the change in the open positions' value from one close to the next, plus what was realised on an exit. Charges are booked on the exit day; slippage of 0.25 pt per fill on the day of the fill. 1 set (65 CE + 65 PE).

    These are paper trades, not real orders. No money was at risk. Not advice.
