How it works

The weekly rules in four steps, one real day, and the monthly idea.

  1. 1

    Expiry day only

    Trade only on the day the NIFTY weekly options expire. That is Tuesday, or the trading day before it when Tuesday is a holiday. No trade on any other day.

  2. 2

    Measure the straddle at 09:25

    At 09:25, add the price of the at-the-money CE and the at-the-money PE. That sum is the straddle, W.

  3. 3

    Sell two options

    Sell the CE at the strike nearest NIFTY + half the straddle, and the PE at the strike nearest NIFTY − half. What you receive is the credit.

  4. 4

    Stop at 2× or exit at 15:15

    If CE + PE together cost 2× the credit, buy both back. Otherwise buy both back at 15:15. LEG-20 and LEG-40 differ only here: a leg that rises 20 or 40 points is bought back alone.

The monthly idea

Sell one strangle for the whole month instead of one each week. At the close of the month's first trading day, sell the CE one straddle above NIFTY and the PE one straddle below. Hold both to the month's last expiry. If CE + PE close at 2× the credit or more, buy both back at the next day's open. It needs no paid data, because every decision uses end-of-day prices, but it holds the position overnight.
